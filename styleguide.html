FAQ

How does the cooling work?

A pressure-activated cooling gel absorbs and disperses body heat — no water, no refrigeration, no electricity. It recharges itself after a few minutes unused.

Is it safe if my pet chews it?

The gel is non-toxic and the cover is durable, but like any mat it's not a chew toy. We include care guidance in the box.

What sizes are available?

Three sizes — Small, Medium and Large — sized to breed weight ranges shown on the product page.
